**Proposed stage 2, between Hidden Garden and Lost in the Green.** Peach walls, long colonnades, window rhythm, grey glass blocks and scaffolding are recognizable motifs.

Proposed use: a short recognizable architecture/ledge passage where the same digital faults first alter an ordinary route. A stable doorway/window rhythm gives a landmark before a ledge misaligns or a passage disappears. A repeated window rhythm helps continuity; a distinctive passage gives the player a landmark. Do not turn every window into a platform or assume scaffolding requires a new climbing system.

Sources C01–C02 and C15–C19 in [source-index.json](source-index.json). Crop human subjects, signs and incidental clutter. The collage consolidates angles and reconstructs details; use original photos later if exact architecture matters.
