**A separate location board, with less emphasis than the lake stage.** Peach walls, long colonnades, window rhythm, grey glass blocks and scaffolding are recognizable motifs.

Proposed uses: a distant skyline behind the garden, an opening/closing courtyard, or a later industrial route. A repeated window rhythm helps continuity; a distinctive passage gives the player a landmark. Do not turn every window into a platform or assume scaffolding requires a new climbing system.

Sources C01–C02 and C15–C19 in [source-index.json](source-index.json). Crop human subjects, signs and incidental clutter. The collage consolidates angles and reconstructs details; use original photos later if exact architecture matters.
