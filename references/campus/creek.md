**A separate later stage, after the rusty crossings and before Toxic Garden.** The creek's focus is the crossing stones, low banks, water and reflections. The grassy channel / confusing vegetation belongs to [Lost in the Green](lost-in-the-green.md).

## What the different angles contribute

| View | Visible reference | Proposed translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approach | One line of low blocks between broad bank steps | Reveal a gently changing route across water |
| Along the stones | Offset edges, gaps, changing apparent heights | A rising/falling walking rhythm rather than a square-tile grid |
| Water / reflections | Flow, broken light and tree silhouettes | Water FX and reflection clues on separate masks |
| Reverse angle | The same crossing seen from the opposite bank | Check silhouette continuity and a recognizable destination |

**The stones inspire the rhythm; they do not dictate a block-shaped level.** Alexander wants repeated broad up/down contours, like the flowing landscape qualities studied in [Planet of Lana](../games/planet-of-lana/layers-and-motion.md). Round banks, shallow dips, generous footholds and a few small gaps can produce that feeling in pixel art. The latest preference allows Hollow Knight/Dead Cells-like exploration/action here as well; a quiet or hiding-focused version remains an alternative. Do not lock the creek into a mandatory precision-jump gauntlet or assume every image glitch changes its ground.

The photos do not demonstrate the proposed hills or game jump distances. Earlier terrain schematics are retained as optional ideas in the proposal appendix, not the current visual brief. Test a continuous slope profile and flat reflection/interaction patches before final art; keep painted grass, collision and water FX separate.
