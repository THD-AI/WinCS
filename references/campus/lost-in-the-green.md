**This is now its own location**, between campus architecture and rusty crossings. It combines the grassy channel / high-low path references with the leafy screening of the earlier Lost in the Green study. The stepping-stone crossing moves to the later [creek](creek.md).

| Look at | Stage possibility | Preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open grassy trough and narrow water | Gentle rises and dips, an occasional glimpse of the way ahead | Flowing banks and walking room |
| High and low paths | A bounded wrong turn, a revealed shortcut | One recognizable landmark and a way back |
| Repeated trunks and leaf screens | Navigation uncertainty; optional line-of-sight puzzle | Player feet, safe landings and exit cues |
| Partly hidden water | Continuity with the lake and later creek | Distinct water / ground silhouettes |

**Proposed play rhythm:** enter from a familiar building edge → lose the broad sightline → follow a changed reflection or landmark → regain a clear view at the crossing. Stealth is a possible variant, not an implied enemy-AI requirement. “Lost” should reward observation rather than hide mandatory collision.

**Layer palette:** distant canopy → grassy far bank / water → middle bushes → walkable curved bank → sparse front trunks/reeds. Keep the path plane stable when decorative foliage moves.

Source family C20–C22. Direct originals C20/C22 ground the merge; previous generated boards supply selected grass/path motifs and leafy mood only. Extra cascades and fantasy stone rows from the oldest draft are excluded. [Generation record](generation.md) · [World route](../../docs/proposal/campus-game.md).
