## What was actually checked

All 59 images and five professor screenshots were inspected in local numbered contact sheets; selected campus, garden, playground-interior, stone and robot originals were inspected at larger size. Every final collage was visually reviewed for its location identity, label legibility, unwanted people, usable motifs and separation requested by Alexander. The generated files are copied into this repository; [media manifest](media-sources.json) stores their hashes and dimensions.

AI cleanup changes details, perspective, vegetation and arrangements. Keep these boards as design references. They are not faithful archival composites or exact floor plans. Rust swatch labels on the crossings board are not a scientific material legend. The hidden-garden water and Toxic Garden passages require stage interpretation; neither proves unphotographed geography.

The 59 photos are owner-supplied, with third-party buildings, objects and occasional people depicted. No blanket release or runtime license is inferred. Final stage art should be original, reviewed and consistent with organizer requirements. Existing-game/trailer media retains its original rightsholders and is never a game asset by default.
