![Small wheeled campus robot reference collage](images/campus-robot.png)

**Keep it cute.** Alexander clarified “an item”: a funny collectible character with companion or upgrade possibilities. It is not a knight or a night-simulation requirement.

Recognizable cues: boxy body, four little wheels, paired round sensors, top lidar disc, exposed wires and small orange module. The collage is an interpretation of photographed angles, not an engineering drawing; reconstructed opposing views may not match real hardware.

Proposed jam version: one small collectible sprite and a happy beep when found. A companion version could follow a simple fixed offset, but needs stuck/reset/transition handling. An upgrade could reveal one optional item or route cue. Choose one role; collecting a funny robot does not require building an inventory, autonomous navigation and an upgrade tree.

Sources C55–C59. Hands, people and institutional lettering were removed from the generated board. Keep exact hardware/brand replication out of the runtime brief.
