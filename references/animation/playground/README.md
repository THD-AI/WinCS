## A familiar playground, occupied by something else

**Visual interpretation:** play equipment stays recognizable while scale, creature occupancy and restricted light change its meaning. Rope grids can frame danger; a tube can conceal a reveal; a small warm opening can look safe before the surrounding space is understood. Colored toys and confetti stop the setting becoming generic darkness.

**Our original stage seed:** keep the photographed tower, ramp and crawl-hole vocabulary. A locally aligned interior plate appears when the student approaches a tube; one unfamiliar shadow moves behind the net. This is optional after the lake slice, not an instruction to copy the animation’s creatures or build its scenes. Interior masks must preserve player position and known collision.
