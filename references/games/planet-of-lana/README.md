## Design interpretation

The following is our analysis for a tiny prototype, not a claim about the developer's internal architecture.

**Loop:** Observe the environment, understand a threat or puzzle, coordinate actions and move forward. Story is expressed through spaces and the companion relationship.

**Dependencies:** Companion command/follow behavior, puzzle state, stealth and cinematic transitions each introduce failure states. A beautiful empty panorama does not automatically create an interactive puzzle.

**Worldbuilding:** Nature versus machine shapes and a consistent horizon can establish a conflict before dialogue. Use one contrast or repeated visual motif, not extensive lore text.

## Jam experiment and question

Borrow scale, negative space and a clear focal point. Use one simple original environmental interaction; no companion AI in the baseline. Alexander admired its visuals rather than naming it as a favorite.

Which single composition helps players see their next action without a quest arrow?

For our own asset organization, group player states, terrain, interaction props and feedback separately, with fixed pivots and explicit frame durations. That is a proposed export contract, not the original game's source folder structure. Before making a whole sheet, test one frame at actual camera scale.
