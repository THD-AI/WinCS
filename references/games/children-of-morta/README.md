## Design interpretation

The following is our analysis for a tiny prototype, not a claim about the developer's internal architecture.

**Loop:** Fight through danger, return to a family context, and let relationships change the meaning of repeated excursions.

**Dependencies:** Multiple playable family members multiply movesets, animation and balance. Combat crowds need attack tells, hit/hurt states and clear depth sorting. Persistent character progression adds save and reset complexity.

**Worldbuilding:** The emotional frame matters as much as the dungeon. A short returning-home beat can make a tiny excursion feel consequential without a family saga.
