[Existing visual views and credits](visual-reference.md) show detailed pixel characters, strong focal colors and magical combat effects. The [developer/publisher listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/330020/) establishes the source; it does not expose sprite sheets or a production pipeline.

**Design observation:** bright concentrated effects can describe magical force against darker terrain. Keep the player shape and important threats readable through those effects. A good-looking magic system does not require importing its skill tree or character roster.

**Proposed original test:** one short spell/interaction effect with anticipation, a clear active shape and a fast fade. Reuse its palette and timing for the lake response only if that fits the chosen direction. A still cannot establish duration; tune it in the prototype and compare against source footage before claiming a timing model.

The top-down/oblique camera is not the platforming camera for WinCS. Family narrative, persistent rosters, homecoming and procedural combat loops are outside this reference's requested role.

Research updated 22 October 2026. Observations and WinCS translations below are design analysis, not extracted development files or accepted game requirements. Reference media remains with its credited rightsholders; no runtime reuse license is inferred.
