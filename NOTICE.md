# Rights and provenance

[← Previous](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [🏠 Home](README.md) · [🖼️ Visual library](references/README.md) · [Next →](README.md)

This private repository contains original planning text and third-party reference media. No public open-source license has yet been selected for the eventual game. Team ownership, contributor credit and publication terms must be agreed before a public release.

Downloaded commercial game screenshots, GIFs and promotional cards remain copyright of their respective developers/publishers. Source pages, direct image URLs, credits, collection dates and hashes are recorded alongside them. They support private design analysis only. Their presence does not grant permission to reuse them as sprites, textures, music, dialogue, characters or marketing for our game.

No development project, commercial sprite sheet, game binary or source code was extracted. A future build must exclude references/ and use original or independently licensed runtime assets with a separate manifest. Review redistribution permission before making the reference library public; replacing images with source links may be appropriate.

The supplied personal chat screenshot and raw transcript are not included. The team brief is a minimal planning digest. Voyager remains a separate project and contributes no runtime assets or source code here.

The [campus library](references/campus/README.md) adds eight active AI-cleaned interpretations of owner-supplied photos. The 59 originals, five professor screenshots, ten recordings and unredacted transcription stay outside Git. [Generation notes](references/campus/generation.md), prompts and source/output hashes distinguish observed motifs from reconstructed details. No exact-map, identity release or runtime reuse permission is inferred. The voice-note document is an edited design digest with recognition uncertainties marked. The selected Playground trailer frames retain Rodrigo Goulão de Sousa's authorship, with music/sound credited to Jérémy Ben Ammar. Game footage captures credit both the game rightsholder and the recorder where applicable. Lana's two process GIFs are published developer demonstrations, not extracted project files. Full source videos stay outside Git. These references are not our artwork.

The [scene-first production study](references/production/master-first/README.md) adds an original generated scene, a visual-smear edit and an unapproved alpha-extraction trial derived from the owner-photo-based boards. Prompts, hashes, dimensions and observed limits are recorded. These are planning examples, not a shipped level, a verified solvable maze or approved runtime layers. Earlier simplified schematics remain optional exploratory material, separate from the current visual brief.
