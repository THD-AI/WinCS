## Brief

Region 0, the tutorial. Scene from the Hidden Garden collage: reed beds, stone-slab paths, a small timber deck and a still pond across the lower third, mirrored like Kingdom Two Crowns' water. Late afternoon, calm, a little too quiet. FOCUS, centre of the frame: DEXTER, a small black panther plush mascot in a light-grey T-shirt (1.5 ft, knee height to the hero), stands on the timber deck at the water's edge, one paw pointing down at the pond. The hero stands two steps behind him on the left, small. In the pond right below Dexter, ONE rectangular patch of the reflection is clearly WRONG: instead of reeds it reflects a rusty blue-green girder maze that does not exist in the scene, its edges torn into glitchy pixels and faint cyan light. The rest of the reflection is normal. Uncanny but beautiful.
