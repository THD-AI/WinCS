## Brief

The first image IS our game's stage, Rusty Creek: keep its composition, the blue-green riveted rusty girder maze, ladders, autumn trees and creek exactly, only widen it to 16:9 and finish it as a real game screenshot. Place the hero from the second image (the THD poncho character) small, walking right along the lowest broad girder deck in the left third of the frame. Add a darker foreground band of autumn leaves and a rusty railing along the bottom edge, gentle haze on the far trees, sunlight glinting on the creek, a few drifting leaves. This image will be the reference that every other image of the game must match.
