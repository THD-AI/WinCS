## Brief

Match the first image's style exactly. Boss arena: the open boss clearing at the end of Rusty Creek (fifth image) - a broad flat girder floor with the creek below. Boss: JENNY, THD's autonomous Formula Student race car from the photos - carbon black and dark-grey bodywork with lime-green, teal and blue stripe graphics, wide front wing, big rear wing, slick tyres, roll hoop - DRIVING ITSELF: the cockpit is EMPTY (no driver, no helmet), a camera and lidar sensor sit on the roll hoop, the blue autonomous-status light on the roll hoop is flashing. The anomaly has taken over her self-driving: headlight-like sensors glow, a little black fluid seeps from the panel seams. She is mid-charge toward the hero with speed lines and tyre smoke; springs and bolts fly off. The hero from the sixth image (small) jumps over her. A thin boss health bar at the bottom of the screen, no text. Huge, readable boss silhouette like Terraria's Wall of Flesh fight.
