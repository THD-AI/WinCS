**Review:** 🟡 The boss read is right: a huge crashing car, debris, goo, the hero jumping it, a boss bar. But the car came out black/gold with a '78' and isn't Fast Forest's green and black. The boss sprite board fixes the livery from the team-banner photos.

## Brief

Match the first image's style exactly. Boss arena: the open boss clearing at the end of Rusty Creek (fourth image) - a broad flat girder floor with the creek below. Boss: JENNY, the green-and-black Formula Student race car from the team banner, haunted - glowing headlight eyes, black goo leaking from the bodywork, crooked front wing - mid-charge toward the hero with speed lines and smoke, springs and bolts flying off. The hero (small) is jumping over it. A thin boss health bar at the bottom of the screen with no text. Big readable boss silhouette like Terraria's Wall of Flesh fight: huge, simple to read.
