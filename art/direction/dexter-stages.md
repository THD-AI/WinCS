The relative scale is a little exaggerated (the hero reads slightly small next to the 6 ft stage); the sprite board uses the exact heights from doc 04.

## Brief

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, NO LABELS, NO MEASUREMENT LINES. Only FIVE figures standing on one ground line on a plain warm-grey backdrop, side views, at TRUE relative scale, in the first image's pixel style and light. From left: (1) the hero from the second image, 6 ft tall - the ruler. (2) DEXTER as the black panther plush mascot from the photos (black velvet fur, ice-blue eyes, orange nose, small fangs, light-grey T-shirt with faint crest, pale grey trousers), TINY: only 1.5 ft, his head reaches the hero's KNEE. (3) Dexter grown to 4 ft (up to the hero's chest): limbs longer, one seam splitting, a little black goo at the joints. (4) Dexter at 6 ft, as tall as the hero, mascot-costume size, T-shirt torn, goo dripping. (5) Dexter FINAL FORM, 8.5 ft - his head is far above the hero's head, lanky uncanny panther-humanoid, black glossy goo leaking from every seam and joint like the Greed in Kingdom Two Crowns (last image), ice-blue light glowing through the torn seams. Same character throughout, getting bigger and wronger.
