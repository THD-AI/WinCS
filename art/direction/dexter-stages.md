## Brief

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, NO LABELS. Four figures on one ground line, side views, TRUE relative scale, in the first image's pixel style and light, on a quiet late-afternoon campus lawn. (1) The hero from the second image, 6 ft - the ruler. (2) DEXTER as the small black panther plush from the third image (grey T-shirt, ice-blue eyes, orange nose), 1.5 ft, his head at the hero's knee. (3) Dexter as a bigger plush, 4 ft, chest height, stitching slightly strained. (4) Dexter's FINAL FORM: the university's full-size panther MASCOT COSTUME from the fourth photo (big fluffy black panther head with ice-blue eyes, orange nose, white whiskers and fangs, black furry neck sleeve, light-grey crest T-shirt, black furry gloves and feet) but 8.5 ft tall, towering over the hero. It looks friendly and harmless at first glance - a waving university mascot like in Duck Season - and is WRONG on a second look: thick black fluid seeps out exactly where the costume pieces join - the gap between the head and the neck sleeve, the glove cuffs at the wrists, the ankles - and drips down; the head sits very slightly too far forward; the eyes are a little too glossy. Secret horror: subtle, no gore, no monster transformation.
