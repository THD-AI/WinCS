## Brief

ONE single game frame, no panels: the Hidden Garden pond scene from the third image, frozen. The hero (second image) hangs in mid-air above the deck in the knocked-back DAMAGE pose (arms flung out, white hit-flash outline), a few pixel cracks radiating across the frozen picture, which is slightly darkened. In the centre of the screen, ON TOP of everything, an old Windows-style error dialog box drawn in crisp pixel art: grey window with a blue title bar that reads THD.exe and a small X button, a red circle with a white X icon on the left, the line THD.exe has stopped responding. and one OK button. Nothing else - no menus, no loading screens, no captions.
