**Review:** 🟡 Kept the first take: clean → cut → stretch → torn bands across the same scene, hero repeated per band. It shows cuts and stretching well. The 404 void and the crash dialog are built as game FX/UI, not art (the second take added baked-in text labels and was rejected).

## Brief

The same Rusty Creek scene as the second image, showing our GLITCH LANGUAGE in four vertical bands left to right: (1) clean; (2) an image CUT - a rectangular slice of the scene shifted sideways and repeated; (3) STRETCHED pixels - scenery smeared vertically into long streaks; (4) a 404 VOID - a big hole in the world filled with flat light grey and a small broken-image icon in it, edges crawling with torn pixels. IN THE CENTRE, ON TOP of everything: a large old-desktop-style ERROR DIALOG BOX (grey window, blue title bar, a red circle X icon, two blank grey text lines, one OK button) hovering over the scene, and beneath it the hero frozen mid-air in a starfish spin. Eerie, funny, beautiful.
