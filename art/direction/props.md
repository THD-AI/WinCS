## Brief

Match the first image's pixel style and light, laid out as a neat prop sheet on a plain warm-grey backdrop, side views, at TRUE scale relative to the 6 ft hero standing at the left: the cling-film-wrapped muffin, marbled cake slice, foil-wrapped chocolate wafer (no brand text), Leberkaese roll in a brown paper bag, strawberry milk carton, iced-coffee cup, a yellow cafeteria tray, the plywood stacking chair, the yellow town-style sign on its steel pole with the green assembly-point sign, the aluminium pocket-computer suitcase. Each prop copies the real photographed object with its real imperfections - wrinkles, glare, scuffs - not an idealised product shot.
