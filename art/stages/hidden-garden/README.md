# Hidden Garden – stage chunks

The prologue, drawn from the approved [Hidden Garden concept](../../locations/hidden-garden-clean.md) as a flat side elevation with its figures removed. One chunk, `hg-0-0` (2160×720): the bench where Dexter sits at the start (x ≈ 190–320), the stone path and the timber jetty (path top y = 441), the end of the jetty where Dexter points (x ≈ 1290–1323), and one wrong patch in the pond's reflection below it – a rusty girder with a ladder that isn't there. Clean only.

**Layers** ([layers.json](layers.json), `python scripts/split_layers.py art/stages/hidden-garden`): [03 rear](layers/hg-0-0.03-rear.png) · [04 water](layers/hg-0-0.04-water.png) · [05 play](layers/hg-0-0.05-play.png) · [07 foreground](layers/hg-0-0.07-foreground.png). Everything above the path is one rear layer at scroll 1.0, and the foreground also scrolls at 1.0: rectangles and colours would tear the big tree or the reeds at another speed, so sky/far/mid parallax waits for hand-drawn masks.
