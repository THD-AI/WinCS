## Desired player experience

“I enter a strange little place, learn one surprising ability, and return to something I noticed earlier. It is funny on the surface, but the return changes what that place means.”

This preserves discovery, a readable silhouette and a small emotional turn from the Hollow Knight reference without requiring its combat, enormous map, boss animation or progression economy.

## Three options

| Option | Core loop | Why it might fit | Main cost / cut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. One new way back** (recommended starting hypothesis) | Explore three connected rooms; obtain one traversal ability; return through a visible earlier gate; make one final interaction | Closest to Vatsal's stated side-scrolling exploration preference | Controller, camera and gate must feel good; cut combat and boss first |
| B. One-room odd job | Observe a patrol/hazard; move a prop or activate a switch; reveal a funny consequence and finish | Strong chance of a complete funny/deep vignette with fewer assets | Less Metroidvania feel; preserve discovery through one hidden interaction |
| C. Tiny combat excursion | Enter one short route, fight one enemy type, obtain one upgrade, return to an exit | Gives Lex's Soul Knight/combat interest a place | Hitboxes, tells, hurt states and balance cost more; cut random generation and loot |

Choose **one**. Do not combine A's traversal, B's puzzle, C's combat plus Kingdom's economy in the jam.

## Provisional scope for option A

- One controllable character, one biome, three compact rooms or camera regions.
- Walk, jump, one context interaction, one earned movement ability (dash **or** double jump, not both).
- A gate is seen before the ability; it becomes passable after learning it. An explicit return route gives meaning to the unlock.
- One forgiving hazard type; retry at the current room entry; no lives, currency, skill tree or save system.
- One opening visual beat, one short funny interaction, one final changed detail that gives it meaning. No branching dialogue system.
- Start instructions, keyboard controls, pause/mute, death/reset and win/restart. Mobile and controller are stretch targets only if required.
- No boss, procedural generation, multiplayer, crafting, shops, quests, 3D, day/night system or hardware port.

## Example original premise seeds (discard freely)

1. A very small courier is repeatedly told a package is too important to open. Returning by a new route reveals that the “urgent delivery” was a small act of care.
2. A maintenance worker in a ridiculous underground institution earns permission to use a shortcut, then discovers the rule was inconveniencing everyone. Humor comes from signs and behavior; depth from the final choice of whom to help.
3. “Memory of a goldfish” could concern remembering a route or helping a forgetful neighbor. It is only a naming seed: an actual memory-reset mechanic would change level design, UI and testing substantially and must replace another system.

These are not claims about the event theme or Vatsal's preferred story. Do not make the game about goblins merely because of the team name.

## Acceptance criteria for a complete tiny game

A new player can open the agreed URL, understand the goal, move reliably, discover the ability, pass the gate, finish and restart without developer help. Death never traps the player. Collision and artwork agree. The build contains no unlicensed reference media. Three outside players try it; record where they get lost and what changed.
