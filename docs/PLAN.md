## Decision gates

**Gate A – today:** ask Vatsal which Hollow Knight qualities matter: exploration, movement, combat, atmosphere, mystery or boss challenge. Ask him to rank two, not approve all six. Lex chooses a voluntary asset budget; Alexander chooses actual support availability.

**Gate B – organizer:** confirm AI tools, generated media, reusable starter code/assets, pre-event research, submission format/host and exact deadline. Until then, organize references and documents; do not assume generated game assets or prewritten gameplay are accepted.

**Gate C – first 45 minutes after reveal:** pick one option in the proposal, tailor it to the theme, lock a win condition and one signature verb. Record changes in the decision log.

**Gate D – first two sprint hours:** a greybox must run in the delivery browser with movement, interaction and restart. Cut scope if setup consumes this gate. A project that only has art and menus fails the gate.

**Gate E – Friday evening:** one complete rough playthrough must exist. Saturday is for completion, feedback and packaging, not discovering the game's core mechanic.
