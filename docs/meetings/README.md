## 23 Oct in one paragraph

The campus starts familiar and gets uncanny, with no gore and no apocalypse, because a quantum-computing experiment broke it. We build the hero and one level first. Rusty Creek and Toxic Garden are the favourites; the Hidden Garden pond is how you fall in. Dexter, the panther plush, guides you and secretly feeds on every anomaly you defeat. Lex's merged idea, a 3-charge glitch meter, sits on top. Controls are four directions plus two buttons. Lex owns stage design on top of the generated art; Alexander owns the pipeline, the build and this document set.
